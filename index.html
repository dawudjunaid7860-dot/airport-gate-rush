How to Play 🧭

  • Tap a passenger, then tap the gate with the same color as their ticket. They hop over and board.
  • Fill every seat before the plane's timer runs out — planes leave on time, full or not!
  • Watch the patience bar under each passenger. Green is chill, red means they're about to storm off angry.
  • 3 wrong sends = game over. Storm-offs count against you too — 5 and your shift ends.
  • Chain correct boardings for combos and bonus points. Full planes pay out coins.

Special Passengers

  • VIP Gold border, double points — but almost no patience. Board them first!
  • FAMILY ×3 Three matching travelers who arrive together. Board all 3 for a bonus.
  • ? Confused travelers switch ticket color once — check the color again before you send them!
